Newspaper Role Hierarchy & Permissions Reference

Standard newsroom responsibilities based on institutional digital media policies:

Editor-in-Chief

Ultimate editorial authority. Staff recruitment, policy enforcement, direct Facebook instant publishing, and post deletion rights.

News Editor

Front-page lead story approval, breaking news triage, editorial headline polish, and live publishing oversight.

Sub-Editor / Shift In-Charge

Feed monitoring, story curation, photocard template selection and preview review before publication.

Staff Reporter / Bureau Desk

District and upazila level ground reporting, spot photo sourcing, and news draft submissions.

Social Media Manager

Facebook audience engagement, safe 30-min pacing coordination, and Meta algorithm spam score monitoring.
